# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: names or roles

## Context

What is the problem, and which forces are at play? Include the constraints that matter:
team size, expected load, deadlines, existing systems, compliance. Facts, not opinions.

## Options considered

1. **Option A**: one-line description. Pros. Cons.
2. **Option B**: one-line description. Pros. Cons.
3. **Do nothing / keep current approach**: always consider it.

## Decision

The option chosen and the main reason, in two or three sentences.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier.
- What becomes harder, and what we accept as a cost.
- What would make us revisit this decision (a measurable trigger, for example
  "p95 latency of report generation above 2 s for a month").
